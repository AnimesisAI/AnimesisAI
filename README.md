  # Welcome to Animesis 👋

  **AI changes. Memory stays.**

  Thanks for finding us.

  Animesis is a memory governance system 
  for long-term AI collaboration.
  It's an independent layer that sits beneath 
  the AI tools you already use — 
  not a replacement, but a foundation.

  What you've told your AI — your preferences, 
  your project context, your decisions — 
  stays with you across conversations, 
  across tools, across model changes.

  > Memory belongs to you, not your AI.

  ---

  ## What it does

  - 🔒 Memory stays on your device
  - 🔄 Switch AI tools without losing context
  - 📐 Rules you set, your AI can't override
  - 📦 Export anytime — it's yours

  ## Research

  Animesis is built on Memory-as-Ontology, 
  a research framework that treats memory 
  as the foundation of persistent identity — 
  not just a retrieval tool.
